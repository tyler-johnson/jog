jog branches — every branch's snapshot chain

usage:
  jog branches [--json]

One row per chain: the branch name, how many snapshots the chain
holds, and the newest snapshot's age and provenance. The first column
marks the branch: * is the current branch, - is a deleted one. Chains
outlive their branches — a deleted branch's chain stays listed until
its snapshots age out of the keep window, and "jog trim --gone" drops
it right away. Work snapshotted on a detached HEAD appears as
@detached.

"jog branch" is an alias of branches.

options:
  --json   machine-readable: branch, live, snapshots, and the newest
           snapshot's id, sha, time, age, and provenance
